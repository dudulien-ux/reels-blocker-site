Reels Blocker ✨

Instagram without the endless scroll.

What it does

  • Hides the Reels tab from Instagram
  • Blocks navigation to Reels
  • Everything else works normally
  • Free & open-source

Why?

Reels are designed to keep you scrolling forever. I wanted to use Instagram without that friction—so I made this extension. It's just a simple tool that removes the temptation.
